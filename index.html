How to use it

Open the file in Word or Google Docs, replace every bracketed field with your company's details, and adjust the scope and pricing sections to match the job. The shaded reference boxes throughout each template show typical industry ranges, not requirements, so confirm your state's actual licensing rules and your insurance policy's actual limits before you finalize a contract. Have a licensed attorney in your state review the contract language before you send it to a client.

Questions

Are these templates really free?
Yes. Download any or all three, edit them, and use them for as many proposals as you need.
Which one do I use if a contract needs both armed and unarmed guards?
Start with the Armed template. Its staffing table has a guard type column, so you can list armed and unarmed posts on the same plan.
Do the pricing figures replace my own quote?
No. They're typical market ranges to sanity-check against, not a quote. Set your own rate based on your local labor cost, insurance load, and margin.
Does this replace a lawyer-reviewed contract?
No. These are proposal templates to speed up your sales process. Have your final contract terms reviewed by a licensed attorney before signing.
